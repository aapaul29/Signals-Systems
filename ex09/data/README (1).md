# Wrist signals during exercise used in the Week 9 coding task C3

`wrist_bike_32hz.csv`: the wrist PPG and the magnitude of the wrist acceleration (both normalized to unit standard deviation) of record `s1_high_resistance_bike`, about 10 min of cycling at high resistance, reduced from 256 Hz to 32 Hz. `ecg_beats.csv`: the time of every heartbeat detected in the chest ECG of the same record and the heart rate of the interval ending there, used as the reference. Written by `make_09d_data.py` from the in-class data.

Source: Wrist PPG During Exercise v1.0.0, PhysioNet, https://physionet.org/content/wrist/1.0.0/
